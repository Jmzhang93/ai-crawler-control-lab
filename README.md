Free llms.txt and AI robots.txt generator for site owners, SEO teams, and developers.

Sitemap:

https://ai-crawler-control-lab.pages.dev/sitemap.xml

## What It Does

AI Crawler Control Lab helps website owners create practical AI crawler policies:

- generate an `llms.txt` draft
- generate AI-focused `robots.txt` rules
- compare GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, and other crawler tokens
- separate AI search visibility from model-training opt-out
- avoid blocking useful search/user-triggered crawlers by mistake
